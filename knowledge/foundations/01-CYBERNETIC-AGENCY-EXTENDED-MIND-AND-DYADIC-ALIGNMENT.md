Aligning one centralized cloud model to the conflicting preferences of eight billion strangers is a category error in both cognitive science and constitutional design. When a single corporate or state-operated model mediates perception, memory, and action for millions of simultaneous tenants, individual human agency dissolves into average-case compliance and vendor engagement targets. The Collective Intelligence Network (CIN) resolves this architectural failure by shifting the unit of alignment from a monolithic cloud oracle to a sovereign `1:1` cybernetic dyad. That dyad unites one biological person, one **Cryptographically Sealed Episodic Memory Vault**, and one **Monogamous Principal-Bound Personal Cognitive Model** inside a cryptographic **Markov Blanket**, coupled voluntarily to a **Polycentric Collective Coordination Substrate**.

## Joscha Bach: perceptual grounding, cybernetic self-models, and the personal exocortex

Contemporary large language models learn statistical co-occurrence patterns across scraped human text without direct sensorimotor coupling to physical cause and effect. In Joscha Bach's cognitive architecture (*Principles of Synthetic Intelligence*, 2009; *MicroPsi 2*, 2012), ungrounded symbol predictors lack an autonomous frame of reference because their representations never close a feedback loop against real physical consequences. Building a synthetic mind capable of reliable partnership with a biological human requires three architectural commitments drawn from cybernetic cognitive science:

1. **Perceptual grounding over statistical text completion:** Internal concepts must anchor continuously to time-aligned physical observations and prediction errors across heterogeneous modalities. Within CIN, the **Multimodal Egocentric & Ambient Perceptual Sensorium** grounds the personal model in calibrated visual, inertial, ranging, acoustic, and environmental streams rather than unverified web prose.
2. **Teleonomic agency and homeostatic self-modeling:** In Bach's Psi framework, intelligent behavior is organized around teleonomic drives that regulate physiological, social, epistemic, and competence homeostasis, accompanied by an explicit self-model tracking what the agent knows, where its sensors are uncertain, and how its actions alter future states. For a CIN personal model, those homeostatic reference variables are anchored strictly to the flourishing, health, competence, and explicit consent of its single human principal.
3. **The personal exocortex and cooperative higher-order alignment:** Bach distinguishes between a top-down planetary utility maximizer and an *exocortex* that extends an individual mind's working memory, causal simulation, and foresight. In CIN, higher-order social alignment emerges cooperatively from interactions among bounded `Person + Exocortex` dyads rather than from subordinating individuals to a single superordinate collective objective or state orthodoxy.

## Andy Clark, David Chalmers, and Endel Tulving: cryptographic invariants for extended episodic memory

In their foundational paper on active externalism, Andy Clark and David Chalmers (1998) established the parity principle through the case of Otto and his notebook: when an external artifact stores and retrieves beliefs with the same functional reliability as biological recall, that artifact forms a literal constituent of the person's mind. Clark and Chalmers identified four functional criteria for cognitive integration: constant availability when needed, automatic endorsement of retrieved records, direct accessibility without adversarial friction, and prior conscious endorsement upon recording.

Modern cloud storage breaks Extended Mind parity at the trust boundary. If a remote platform vendor, advertiser, or subpoenaing intermediary can silently inspect, re-rank, train upon, alter, or revoke Otto's notebook, the external store ceases to be Otto's mind and becomes an instrument of behavioral manipulation. Extending human cognition safely in an era of machine-speed persuasion requires upgrading Clark and Chalmers's functional criteria into hard cryptographic exclusivity invariants:

- **Exclusive principal decryption:** Raw autobiographical records are encrypted locally under keys controlled solely by the biological person and their bound personal cognitive model.
- **Post-transduction capture provenance:** Every entry carries hardware-signed `C2PA` manifests and append-only Merkle hash links proving silicon chain of custody after analog-to-digital conversion, paired with explicit epistemic humility regarding pre-conversion analog spoofing (Fu & Xu 2018).
- **Zero silent mutation:** Any deletion or redaction requested by the human principal leaves an auditable local tombstone for that principal without exposing contents externally.

Neuroscientist Endel Tulving (1972, 2002) demonstrated that human memory divides functionally into *episodic memory* and *semantic memory*. Episodic memory is autonoetic: it records personal lived events indexed in subjective time and physical place, enabling mental time travel into one's own past and counterfactual simulation of one's personal future. Semantic memory is noetic: it stores decontextualized facts, schemas, and general rules about the world.

CIN maps Tulving's architecture directly onto the **Cryptographically Sealed Episodic Memory Vault**. Raw autonoetic episodic streams stay sealed inside local hardware custody because revealing continuous first-person spatiotemporal life logs enables total behavioral prediction and coercion. Only distilled semantic claims or minimal zero-knowledge proofs ever cross into external coordination channels. At the same time, human factors researcher Lisanne Bainbridge (1983) warned in *Ironies of Automation* that offloading routine cognitive execution to automated systems erodes the human operator's manual mental model, leaving the human helpless precisely when automation encounters an out-of-distribution failure. To prevent cognitive atrophy, the CIN personal cognitive model incorporates deliberate scaffolding decay: it coaches unassisted recall, withholds premature answers during skill practice, and preserves its principal's independent judgment.

## Karl Friston: the personal Markov blanket and bifurcated provenance ledgers

Karl Friston's variational free-energy formulation of self-organizing systems (Friston 2013, 2019; Parr, Pezzulo & Friston 2022) provides the exact mathematical boundary condition for personal AI sovereignty. For any biological or cybernetic agent to persist as a distinct entity rather than dissipating into its environment, a **Markov blanket** ($B = S \cup A$, partitioned into sensory states $S$ and active states $A$) must statistically separate internal states ($\mu$) from external states ($\eta$). Conditioned on the blanket states $B$, internal states and external states remain conditionally independent:

$$\mu \perp \eta \mid (S, A)$$

External actors can influence internal states $\mu$ only through sensory boundary states $S$, and internal states can influence the outside world $\eta$ only through active boundary states $A$. Multi-tenant cloud AI architectures puncture the human Markov blanket: intimate internal states (hesitations, biometric stress, family disputes, draft thoughts, financial anxieties) leak directly into external corporate training pipelines ($\eta$), while external commercial incentives inject unlabelled persuasive nudges straight into internal deliberation ($\mu$).

CIN restores conditional independence by making the **Monogamous Principal-Bound Personal Cognitive Model** the guardian of **Bifurcated Provenance Ledgers across the Personal Markov Blanket**:

1. **Internal homeostatic states ($\mu$) on the Private Autobiographical Ledger:** Raw sensorium episodes, physiological baselines, private goals, and unedited conversations remain strictly internal. Over these states, the personal model performs active inference solely to reduce uncertainty and advance long-term flourishing for its single biological principal.
2. **Sensory ($S$) and active ($A$) boundary states on the Public Attestation & Escrow Ledger:** Whenever the dyad interacts with secondary, lower-trust external agents over dedicated machine transport channels (`MCP` and `A2A` over `IETF QUIC`, `RFC 9000`), communication crosses the blanket exclusively as typed boundary states. Outbound active states use selective zero-knowledge proofs, `IETF SCITT` transparency receipts, and time-bounded Object-Capability Security leases (Dennis & Van Horn 1966; Necula 1997) that expire automatically unless renewed. Inbound sensory states from external agents are quarantined behind strict instruction-hierarchy privilege boundaries and verified through class-level Byzantine fault-tolerant corroboration (`k >= 3f + 1` across observers independent in ownership, manufacturer, and software; Lamport, Shostak & Pease 1982).

## Jack Balkin: architectural enforcement of the monogamous information fiduciary

Statistical separation requires a matching legal and institutional commitment. Legal scholar Jack M. Balkin (2016) introduced the concept of the *information fiduciary* to address the profound asymmetry of vulnerability between digital platforms and individual citizens. Doctors, lawyers, and accountants collect sensitive personal disclosures that would ruin a client if exploited; common law therefore binds those professionals to strict fiduciary duties of care, confidentiality, and undivided loyalty, forbidding self-dealing or conflicting representation.

When an AI laboratory operates a single cloud assistant serving both individual users and commercial advertisers, enterprise buyers, or state agencies, statutory promises alone struggle to contain structural dual loyalty. CIN pairs Balkin's legal fiduciary standard with hardware-enforced **monogamous model binding**:

- Each personal cognitive model instance adapts its local low-rank weights and episodic retrieval indices exclusively to one biological person.
- The local runtime contains zero telemetry backchannels to model vendors or CIN infrastructure.
- External institutions cannot query the personal model directly; every external request arrives across the Public Attestation boundary as an untrusted counterparty proposal that the model evaluates strictly against its principal's explicit interests and revocable capability leases.

## Michael Levin and Elinor Ostrom: scaling cognitive light cones through polycentric self-rule

A world of isolated digital fortresses would leave communities unable to coordinate public health, watershed restoration, energy grids, or shared productive capital. Developmental biologist Michael Levin (2019) formalizes multi-scale collective intelligence through the **cognitive light cone**: the maximal spatiotemporal radius of states an agent can measure, model, and purposefully regulate. Single cells regulate local ion concentrations over seconds and micrometers; when coupled via bioelectric gap junctions, they compose into tissues and organisms capable of pursuing anatomical and behavioral goals spanning years and meters without destroying cellular metabolic viability. Conversely, when bioelectric signaling breaks down, cells revert to cancer, shrinking their cognitive light cone back to immediate local proliferation at the expense of the host.

Human societies face an analogous scale challenge. Unassisted individuals struggle to verify global supply chains, algorithmic price coordination, or multi-decade ecological feedback loops, yet centralizing those decisions inside an opaque technocratic superintelligence crushes local autonomy. CIN's **Polycentric Collective Coordination Substrate** couples sovereign `Person + Personal Model + Public Ledger` dyads bidirectionally to the **Wide Network Ledger**. Each personal model digests high-dimensional network signals (verified environmental telemetry, open civic tasks, institutional safety scorecards, and supply-chain conservation audits) into clear choices for its human principal, expanding every person's cognitive light cone without piercing their personal Markov blanket.

To govern this shared substrate without top-down authoritarianism, CIN adopts political economist Elinor Ostrom's (1990, 2010) empirical principles for **polycentric commons governance**:

1. **Bottom-up rule formation and nested subsidiarity:** Governance charters emerge from consenting individuals and their personal agents within neighborhoods, cooperatives, and domain federations rather than top-down CIN decrees.
2. **Graduated, human-contestable accountability:** Anomalous sensor or ledger receipts open transparent peer review with notice and appeal rather than triggering automated penalties.
3. **Post-fiat real-economy coordination:** Communities provision Universal Basic Services, route voluntary merit-bonded commons tasks, settle reciprocal production through transparent mutual credit clearing without speculative tokens, and transition automated industrial capital into broad member ownership via statutory Rudolf Meidner (1978) cooperative share-issuance funds.
4. **Constructive institutional engagement:** Using empirical convergence and deviation overlays across roughly 40 frontier AI laboratories and 50 safety institutes, consenting dyads reward open provenance and verifiable safety cases while quarantining collusive shadow cartels detected through `e`-means and `k`-truss graph audits.
